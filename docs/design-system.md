# ModusFi Website — Design System

## Brand and tone
ModusFi is a sophisticated technology engineering consultancy, with proven financial-services experience and applied AI. Design should feel trustworthy, contemporary, restrained and technically credible—not like a retail banking product.

## Wordmark
Lowercase `modusfi.` with no separate symbol. On light backgrounds, “modus” is navy and “fi.” turquoise; on dark backgrounds, “modus” may be white and “fi.” turquoise. Reuse existing implementation.

## Colours
- Primary navy: `#101D32`
- Accent turquoise: `#00B3C6`
- Primarily white/light backgrounds
- Existing muted blues and greys from site CSS; inspect actual tokens before editing

Do not introduce new brand colours casually. Use turquoise primarily for emphasis, links and selected controls. Maintain accessible contrast.

## Typography
- Headings: Manrope, typically 600; use existing `.heading`, `.hero h1`, `.page-hero h1` conventions.
- Body: Inter, typically 400.
- Avoid heavy heading weights and overly tight tracking.
- Use the existing type scale and responsive rules as the authority; do not create a competing scale without approval.

## Layout and components
- Generous whitespace, clear hierarchy, restrained borders/radius.
- Navy hero and call-to-action/footer treatments; predominantly light content sections.
- Reuse `.container`, `.section`, grid and card patterns already in CSS.
- Keep buttons and navigation visually consistent across pages.
- Ensure responsive layouts and visible keyboard focus.

## Imagery
- Prefer relevant, credible engineering/financial-services imagery to generic fintech clichés.
- Homepage expertise section uses an AI-generated financial-district architectural image (`/images/modusfi-financial-district.webp`).
- Hero uses a restrained dotted/wave technical motif.
- Optimise images, specify dimensions and use suitable alt text.
- Avoid stock trading screens, floating currency symbols, gratuitous AI brains, busy node diagrams and decorative imagery without purpose.

## Copy
- British English, concise, specific and enterprise-oriented.
- Do not exaggerate company credentials or conflate founders' former projects with ModusFi delivery.
- No Team page unless explicitly approved.

## Review checklist
- Does the page match existing typography, colour and spacing?
- Is the layout clear at desktop and mobile widths?
- Are links and form controls keyboard accessible?
- Are images appropriately sized and described?
- Is copy accurate, restrained and consistent?
